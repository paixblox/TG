Research library and evidence limits

Five sources supplied for this project

Open these articles to inspect the actual research and clinical explanations. The research discusses different outcomes; it does not establish a universal harmful effect from water with meals.

1. Nehlig (2022): Coffee and the gastrointestinal tract (scholarly review)Open full article — Coffee has multiple effects on digestive physiology; its relationship with reflux is not simple.
2. Lappalainen et al. (1993): Water with a meal, hunger and satietyOpen PubMed study — A small study of water consumed with a meal and subjective fullness.
3. Pouderoux et al. (1997): Carbonated water and gastric emptyingOpen PubMed study — A small study exploring gastric distribution and emptying.
4. Cleveland Clinic (2023): Hunger pangsOpen clinical explainer — Hunger and stomach sensations have multiple contributing factors.
5. Mayo Clinic: Water during and after mealsOpen clinical explainer — Drinking water with meals generally does not impair digestion.

These references support broad educational statements, not a numerical digestive compatibility score. Clinical outcomes depend on the person and the complete meal. The application does not establish causal effects from a single meal observation.
